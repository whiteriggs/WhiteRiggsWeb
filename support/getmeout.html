FAQ

How much does it cost?

Nothing. GetMeOut is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. There is no sign-up either.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All excuse and evidence generation happens 'on-device' for maximum privacy.

Is it compatible with my iPhone?

Requires iOS 17.0 or later, on iPhone and iPad. On iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence, the credibility score is also computed by the model on your own device.

What languages does it speak?

Spanish and English, excuses included. The app follows your iPhone's language.

Does the fake call look real?

It reproduces the iOS incoming call screen with whatever name you type, plus haptics and a running timer once you answer. It does not use the phone network: it is a screen inside the app, so it costs nothing and leaves no call log.

Total Privacy

Updated: Aug 1, 2026

Zero Collection

GetMeOut collects and transmits no data at all. Your favourites and your history are stored on your iPhone only: no server, no account, no analytics.

Zero Permissions

The app asks for no permissions at all: no photos, no contacts, no location, no notifications. When you share a piece of evidence you do it through the iOS share sheet, and you decide where it goes.

Security by Design

Being 100% offline, your 'excuses' are safe from any external data leaks.

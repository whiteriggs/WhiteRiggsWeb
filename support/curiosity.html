Frequently asked

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Curiosity is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. You don't need to create an account either.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone with iOS 15 or later. The interactive widget and native translation get better on newer versions, but the app works on all of them.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Curiosity keeps a local stash of facts and preloads the next ones in the background, so you can keep discovering on a plane or in the underground. Your saved favourites are always available.

What languages does it speak?

The interface is available in English, Spanish and Catalan, and follows your device language. Facts are published in English and translated on the fly — tap the globe icon to read the original at any time.

Where do the facts come from?

From public science and trivia databases, plus a hand-picked collection bundled with the app. Each fact shows its source, and the app remembers what you have already seen so it doesn't repeat itself.

Can I change the app icon?

Yes. There are 11 icons, one per planetary theme. You can pick one manually or let it follow whichever theme you are using.

Does it collect my data?

No. There is no tracking, no analytics and no account. Your favourites and preferences never leave your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

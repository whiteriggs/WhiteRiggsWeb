LYVARA

Your whole net worth in one calm place.

Coming soon on the App Store

What it does

It gathers what you own and what you owe, values it with official data, and shows you where it is heading.

📊

Your net worth at a glance

One figure at the top: what you own minus what you owe. Below it, how it has moved over time on a chart you can scrub with your finger, plus the split by asset type.

📈

Live investments

Index funds, ETFs, stocks, crypto, savings accounts and term deposits. Prices are fetched automatically, and anything in dollars is converted to euros at the official ECB rate.

🧾

Every move, accounted for

Contributions, withdrawals, dividends and staking, fees included. That is where your real return, your annualised return and your true invested amount come from. If you contribute monthly, the app logs it for you.

🏠

Property from the land registry

Type the address and the app pulls surface, year and cadastral reference, then estimates the value from the official average price in your area. It refreshes itself every quarter. If you only own part of it, you set your share.

🚗

Cars that depreciate on their own

Enter the model, the price when new and the registration date. The value drops year after year following the official Spanish depreciation table, adjusted to real resale prices.

🏦

Mortgages and loans

Outstanding balance, monthly payment, interest and payment day. The app amortises it for you and tells you what you owe today, without digging out the bank's schedule.

🧮

What the taxman would take

An estimate of what you would pay if you sold today, bracket by bracket, and also asset by asset so you can see what is worth touching. Indicative only, not tax advice.

🔭

An honest projection

Projects your portfolio years ahead using real ECB inflation, not made-up numbers. And if you want to try an idea, simulate a portfolio from scratch without touching your own.

✨

An advisor that never leaves your iPhone

Ask it in plain words about your net worth, about funds to diversify with, or what your car will be worth in three years. It answers using your real figures and runs on Apple Intelligence inside the device. It is optional: switch it on or off in Settings.

Where the numbers come from

No reference figure is made up. These are the sources, and the app always tells you the date each figure belongs to.

  • Banco Central Europeo — exchange rates and euro area inflation.
  • Eurostat / INE — House Price Index, used for property appreciation.
  • Ministerio de Vivienda y Agenda Urbana — average appraised value of free-market housing, in euros per square metre by municipality.
  • Dirección General del Catastro — surface, construction year and use of the property.
  • Yahoo Finance — quotes and price history for funds, ETFs and stocks.
  • CoinGecko y Binance — quotes and price history for crypto.
  • Boletín Oficial del Estado — official depreciation table for used vehicles.

LYVARA is not affiliated with any of these institutions. Value estimates are a market reference, not an appraisal: they know nothing about the condition, floor or orientation of your home. Nothing shown in the app is financial advice.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. LYVARA is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. There is no account to create either.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. LYVARA does not connect to any bank and never asks for banking credentials. You enter your data yourself.

My town is missing when estimating value

The official price table only details municipalities above 25,000 inhabitants. Elsewhere the provincial average is used, and the app tells you so. You can always type the value yourself.

My neighbourhood is not worth the town average

True: the official price is the average for the whole town, and within a large city one neighbourhood can be worth twice another. So if you correct the estimated value, the app remembers that gap and keeps it: every quarter it follows your town's market while preserving how far your area sits from the average.

Does my property value update on its own?

Yes, as long as you entered town and surface. When the ministry publishes a new quarter, the app recalculates the value with the updated price. An amount you typed by hand without those details is never overwritten.

Can I change the pre-filled figures?

Yes. Official figures are a sensible starting point, not a rule: any value you type overrides them.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later. It works offline; the internet is only used to refresh quotes and official data. The advisor additionally needs Apple Intelligence switched on.

Does the advisor send my figures anywhere?

No. It uses the Apple Intelligence model already living inside your iPhone, so your questions and your data never leave the device. If you would rather not have it, turn it off in Settings and it disappears from the tab bar.

How often is the official data refreshed?

The app checks once a day. Inflation is published monthly, ECB rates every six weeks and the house price index quarterly, so you always see the latest available figure with its date next to it.

Can I see everything in dollars?

Yes. In Settings you pick euro or dollar and every amount is converted at the day's rate. The same screen lets you switch between five colour themes.

What if I change iPhone?

Your data travels in the device backup, like any app that stores locally. There is no account to create and no password to remember.

Privacy

Your net worth is your business. The app is built so it stays that way.

It stays on your iPhone

Balances, properties, debts and transactions are stored on the device only. There is no server holding your figures.

What leaves the device

Only public lookups: market prices, exchange rates, macro data and the address you type when searching the land registry. Never how much you own.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no profiling, no selling data to third parties.
